## Step 1: Load the voice (before writing a word)

Read my style guide at `~/.claude/STYLE.md` and any files it points to. If there is no style guide, ask me for three or four examples of my own writing to the same kind of reader and work from those.

## Step 2: Get the context

If I gave a thread link, read the thread and anything it links to before drafting. Do not rely on my summary.
If I gave context inline, work from that.
If the audience or intent is unclear, ask one specific question. Do not guess.

## Step 3: Draft from the voice

Match the register to the situation: layered when the situation is layered, short and plain for a quick reply. Complicate flat claims, push back on stock framings, ground points in specific detail. Follow the style guide's rules on punctuation, phrasing and spelling.

## Step 4: Self-check

Go through the style guide's checklist against the draft. If anything fails, redraft. Do not ship a draft that fails its own checklist.

Example checklist (replace with your own):
- No em dashes.
- No "it's not X, it's Y".
- No exclamation marks.
- British English throughout.
- First person kept.
- Nothing restating what the reader already knows.

## Step 5: Show, do not send

Present the draft as a quote block. Do not send it anywhere. Wait for my sign-off.

If I push back on tone, re-read the voice files and rewrite from them. Do not patch word by word.
