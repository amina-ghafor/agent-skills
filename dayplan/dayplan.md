Read the calendar and the backlog, then hand back a single plan for the day. Never write to either source.

## What counts as input

- The calendar: meetings and any blocks already placed for today.
- The backlog: tasks marked Overdue or Due This Week.

## What comes back

- The day's fixed points in order, and the free time between them.
- A backlog task matched to each gap, sized against its own estimate.
- A first task, named, with the reason.
- Anything due this week that will not fit today, flagged now rather than on the day it is actually due.

## How it decides

- No estimate on a task means 30 minutes, stated as an assumption rather than applied silently.
- A full day gets said plainly, along with what has to drop.
- One recommendation only, never a shortlist.
